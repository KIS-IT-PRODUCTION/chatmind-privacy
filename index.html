Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to the ChatMind app (herein referred to as "App" or "Service"), developed and maintained by Nadia Danko ("we", "our", or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our App.

Information We Collect

We may collect information that you provide directly to us, including:

  • Account information (email address, username)
  • Profile information you choose to provide
  • Communications you send to us
  • Chat messages you send when using our AI-powered features
  • Usage data and app interactions

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to provide, maintain, and improve our services, process your messages through third-party AI services, and respond to your questions.

Third-Party AI Services

Our app includes AI-powered features that rely on third-party AI services to function. When you use these features:

  • Service provider: We use OpenAI to process your messages. OpenAI's handling of your data is governed by their Privacy Policy.
  • No server-side storage: We do not store your chat messages on our servers. Conversations exist only during your active session.
  • Data sent: Only the text content of your conversation is sent to OpenAI — no personal identifiers (like email or name) are included.

Data Security

We implement appropriate technical measures to protect your personal information, though no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
